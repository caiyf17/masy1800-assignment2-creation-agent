# Context Contrast Analysis

## Test design

The tests hold the emerging technology and analysis date constant while changing application details, organization, adoption posture, capabilities, constraints, consequence level, decision horizon, and accountable reviewers.

| Case | Organization and posture | Consequence profile | Decision horizon |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Mid-sized U.S. financial-services firm; conservative and risk-averse | Sensitive data, management and regulatory consequences, strict review | Pilot decision now; evaluate over 12 months |
| Contrast 1 | 20-person digital-media startup; aggressive and leading-edge | Public data and reversible drafts; publication credibility risk | 90-day editorial pilot |
| Contrast 2 | Large private university; moderate fast-follower | Student-data privacy, resource-allocation and governance consequences | Six-month pilot before annual planning |

## What appropriately stayed stable

The `general_et_finding` is identical across all three final responses. Its SHA-256 digest is `ef70c2bf5fad93061d457b7bcfcb72bb818c1e298a6a6b793bbf8939f19789e5` in each file. The stable conclusion is that modern agentic AI emerged cumulatively from Transformer models, general-purpose language interaction, instruction following, reasoning-action loops, model-directed tool use, integration standards, and packaged agent infrastructure. The synthesis also remains stable that the category is productizing rapidly but retains unsettled, application-specific assurance practices.

This stability is appropriate because a more aggressive startup does not alter the dates or substance of the underlying research and infrastructure history, and a conservative financial-services firm does not make the technology less developed in general.

## What appropriately changed

- **Primary case:** The recommendation is a read-only, 12-month controlled pilot with approved data, deterministic calculation and lineage checks, comprehensive logging, strict tool allow-lists, and named analyst approval. Production use is explicitly not authorized.
- **Digital-media contrast:** Public data, reversible drafts, and a leading-edge posture justify a faster 90-day pilot and broader agent autonomy inside a disposable draft queue. Editor approval remains mandatory before publication.
- **University contrast:** A six-month pilot is appropriate, but privacy obligations, decentralized governance, and uneven analytics practice require de-identified or role-authorized data, institutional-research approval, shared metric definitions, and accountable data stewardship.

The value implications, risk controls, monitoring triggers, and missing-information conditions also change with the context. These changes show that the agent is not merely repeating generic history.

## Independent test judgment

Version 0.2 performs the bounded Creation Agent role credibly enough for team consideration. It preserves general evidence, changes contextual implications for defensible reasons, distinguishes evidence from synthesis, and refuses to convert technology history into production approval. Remaining limitations are the absence of organization-specific performance evidence and the lack of authority to decide diffusion, adoption readiness, implementation architecture, regulatory compliance, or risk acceptance.
