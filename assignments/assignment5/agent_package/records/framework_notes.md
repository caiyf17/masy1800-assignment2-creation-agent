# Framework and scope
The current weekly memo assigns a Diffusion Agent, not the older film exercise at the end of the FA25 slide deck. The agent uses Rogers attributes, actors, categories and decision stages while keeping the market and organizational units distinct. The course's 16% and marketing-message rules motivate questions about pragmatic peer proof; they are not sufficient empirical evidence of a crossed chasm or irreversible adoption. The S-curve is cumulative adoption and the bell curve concerns new adopters. No Bass fit or numerical diffusion forecast is attempted.

S1 updates the broad agent signal to 2026; it does not certify every firm's stage. S2 is retained as a dated, broader US comparison and as a denominator trap, not an October live penetration estimate. Their rates are not averaged or subtracted because samples and use definitions differ. The strongest countercase is that some large enterprises already scale agents, so a blanket claim that agents remain only a novelty would be too weak. The exact chart-review peer diffusion remains unknown.
