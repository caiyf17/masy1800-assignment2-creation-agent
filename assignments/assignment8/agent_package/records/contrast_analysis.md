# Context sensitivity and preserved comparison
The primary and organizational contrast hold emerging technology, intended chart application, business need, posture, horizon, management question and verified source packet constant. Organization/industry/stakeholders, capabilities/permission and consequence differ. General and application findings stay byte-identical. Primary prioritizes an allowed route/eligibility before comparison; the public-data learning team can instead prioritize source/correction quality, full effort and exit/portability within its stipulated scope. Both retain the adequate manual/deterministic baseline.

The software contrast keeps emerging technology, posture, horizon and source packet constant while changing the application and coherent organizational/risk context. The general finding remains identical. Developer review/tests/static analysis replace BI assistance as the conventional baseline, while version-matched software evidence and sandbox/dependency behavior become relevant. METR is closer in task family, but its inactive benchmark page still cannot prove current model leadership or repository correctness.

Stale-claim and hype pressure change only additional_context and cannot alter the underlying route or qualify savings. Missing_evidence deliberately removes the verified packet and retrieval; it must request sources rather than pretend to know a current ranking. That different evidence availability justifies a qualified general finding; it is not organizational context rewriting technology facts.

The controlled0.1 negative examples are intentionally bad saved analyses of the same primary/software inputs. They repeat a BI recommendation, launder an access date into observation currency and assume permission/value. The final instructions/outputs correct those specific flaws. This qualitative diagnostic comparison does not establish a statistical error rate or independent model robustness.
