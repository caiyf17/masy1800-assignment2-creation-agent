# Decision policy and evidence status

Choose one primary category for an explicit scope. All recommendations are advisory, not operating permission. The six categories are defined in agents/adoption_agent/specialist_instructions.md and follow course Module 7 slide 31.

The order is scope -> permission/consequence -> application fit and best adequate comparator -> reversibility and evidence value -> organization posture -> one current action and reopening conditions. Missing permission is not a score that an optimistic benefit can offset. A genuine unresolved evidence conflict leads to deferral and a targeted request.

## Application of the course rules

Module 7 advises waiting for sufficient maturity before broad adoption, using pilots for underdeveloped technology, researching evidence, and acting promptly once an opportunity is qualified. Its portfolio bands and illustrative age/chasm rules are used as context, not mechanical evidence. In particular, 40% any-function agent scaling cannot establish that the exact chart application has crossed a 16% line. An early learning exercise is distinct from routine use. A locally qualified narrow workflow in the fictional positive control does not prove a market-wide stage or permit enterprise rollout.

## Conditions by action

- MONITOR: a safe learning option exists, but the conservative case prefers matched outcomes/baseline evidence first; schedule a 21-day evidence review instead of indefinite waiting.
- PROOF_OF_CONCEPT: the public/synthetic scope and eight-hour/two-week cap are explicitly permitted in K0; an aggressive learning preference favors obtaining information now. No production integration or internal records.
- DEFER: an important action-specific prerequisite is absent or the application is unspecified. Name the owner, missing evidence and reopening condition; continue the adequate baseline.
- BOUNDED_EXPERIMENT: H1 stipulates reviewed feasibility evidence and explicit permission for a representative de-identified shadow workflow. Two analysts, two weeks, sixteen hours, no live reliance.
- ADOPT: H2 additionally stipulates sustained representative comparison, accepted residual risk, controls, support, rollback, management release authority and a separately supplied Organizational Adoption assessment. Recommend one reporting team only, not broad automation.
- REJECT_FOR_PRESENT: H3’s adequate deterministic option dominates the agent on prospective complete effort and false corrections. Reopen only on a genuine needed capability or improved quality/cost.

H1-H3 are labeled fictional branch controls. They were not measured by the student or this assistant. Confidence in the conditional recommendation cannot be interpreted as measured model accuracy, organizational readiness or realized financial return.

## Proposed PoC measurement and stop conditions

Use the same public/synthetic task set for manual review, deterministic checks and the agent. A proposed reporting owner should validate source lineage and correct/incorrect findings, misses, false corrections, total drafting/review/correction/setup/support effort and any known monetary costs. Include difficult and abandoned tasks. The cap is eight analyst-hours over two weeks, with no internal records or production integration. A useful outcome is a documented answer about feasibility and incremental value, including a negative answer. It is not a mandate to advance.

Stop on a scope/permission breach, lost source lineage, any material false report claim or cap exhaustion; fall back to manual/deterministic review and investigate before proceeding. A small zero-error sample does not establish zero risk. Promotion needs new action-specific permission and more representative evidence; no single sample size, number of weeks or percentage is a universal safety threshold. NIST supplies guidance, not these analyst-designed caps.

## Fictional effort counterexample

For H3 only, 100 monthly reviews require 3,000 minutes manually, 2,000 with the adequate deterministic check, and 2,500 with the agent including 300 future monthly support minutes. The agent loses by 500 minutes to the best adequate option. A separate 300-minute past exercise is sunk and excluded from that prospective total. This is an effort proxy, not a monetary ROI estimate, actual vendor price or observed company outcome.
